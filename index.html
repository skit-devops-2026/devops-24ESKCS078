The Science of Cognitive Architecture

🛠️

Fogg Behavior Model

When your sleep debt is high, OptiRoutine swaps a demanding session for a 5-minute micro-task instead — lowering the bar just enough to keep the streak alive.

🔥

Loss Aversion

Losing feels twice as sharp as winning feels good. OptiRoutine's prompts lean into that — framed around protecting the streak you've already built, not starting a new one.

📌

Implementation Intentions

Set the trigger once — "If it's 6 PM, open the editor" — and the decision is already made before willpower even enters the picture.

📡

Biometric Context

Sleep quality and task performance get correlated over time, so nudges land when your energy is actually there to act on them.
